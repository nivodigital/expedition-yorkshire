How it works

From idea to itinerary in three easy steps

1

Tell us what you love

Coast or countryside, castles or film locations, a relaxed amble or plenty of stops — a quick message or call is all it takes. No forms, no pressure.

2

We craft the itinerary

Your driver-guide shapes a route around your interests, your group and the season — with honest advice on what’s worth your time and what to skip.

3

You travel, we adapt

On the day the plan flexes around you. Linger where you love it, move on where you don’t — it’s your vehicle and your day, start to finish.

Bespoke, not off-the-shelf

A day that fits you, not a timetable

Our set tours are a brilliant starting point, but nothing has to be fixed. Want to add a family-history stop, build the day around a special anniversary, chase the best light for photography or keep the walking gentle? Just say the word. Every Expedition Yorkshire day is private from the outset, so shaping it around you is simply how we work.

Make it yours

What you can shape

Route & region

Dales, Moors, coast, cities or a mix — we build the driving day around where you most want to be.

Pace & timing

An unhurried single stop or a full day of highlights, with pick-up whenever suits you.

Stops & interests

History, literature, film locations, wildlife, gardens or photography — we lean the day into what you love.

Food & drink

Add a country-pub lunch, an afternoon tea or a farm-shop stop — tell us your tastes and we’ll book ahead.

Special occasions

Anniversaries, big birthdays, proposals and family reunions, quietly and thoughtfully arranged.

Group & access

Couples to larger parties across two vehicles, with step-friendly comfort and easy days for every generation.
