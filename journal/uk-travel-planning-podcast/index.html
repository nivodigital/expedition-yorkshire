We were delighted when our founder, Andrew Ward, was invited onto the UK Travel Planning Podcast — a show dedicated to helping visitors plan a trip to Britain, from first-timers to seasoned returnees.

Listen to the episode

In the episode, Andrew shares what Expedition Yorkshire does and what makes exploring Yorkshire and the North of England by private, driver-guided tour so rewarding. You can listen to the full conversation here: Expedition Yorkshire on the UK Travel Planning Podcast.

Planning a trip to Yorkshire?

If the episode leaves you inspired, that is rather the point. Everything we do is private and shaped around you: full-day private tours across Yorkshire, long-distance transfers between Britain’s great cities, and multi-day expeditions for those with more time. Every day is led by local guides who know the county intimately.

To hear more of the thinking behind it, read why private tours are the first choice for US travellers and why long-distance private touring is on the rise — or simply get in touch to start planning your own Yorkshire day.
