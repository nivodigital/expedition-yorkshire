The people who make the day

Meet your driver-guides

Not a rota of strangers — a small team who live here and love showing it off. You'll know who's driving before you arrive.

Andrew

Founder & Lead Driver-Guide

Andrew is one of the UK's most experienced driver-guides, having previously led more than a thousand tours to Stonehenge, Bath, the Cotswolds, Oxford and Kent. In summer 2021 he and his family moved to Yorkshire and Expedition Yorkshire was born — built to show guests the best of Yorkshire and the north of England, at your pace and in your way.

Richard

Senior Driver-Guide

Born, raised and schooled around Hebden Bridge, Richard has lived his whole life in the North of England and is proud of his Yorkshire roots. A family dentist in Malton for over 30 years, he's now our Senior Guide — guests enjoy his local accent as much as his local knowledge — and also guides at Scampston Hall and on walking tours of York.
