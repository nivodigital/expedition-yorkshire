In short

One vehicle, chosen with care

Every private tour and transfer runs in a Mercedes-Benz V-Class — a premium seven-seat people carrier chosen for comfort, space and those all-important big windows. It seats up to seven guests alongside your driver-guide, with no one else aboard, and is fully licensed by York City Council. Kept spotless inside and out, it's the ideal way to see Yorkshire without the stress of driving, parking or navigating.
