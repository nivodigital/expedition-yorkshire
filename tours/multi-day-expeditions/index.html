Ways to build your expedition

There's no fixed itinerary — most expeditions start from one of these shapes and are then tailored around you.

1

Two days

The Expedition Double

Choose any two of our Yorkshire day tours and pay a little less for each — say All Creatures one day, Whitby and the coast the next.

2

Three days

The Expedition Triple

Two Yorkshire days plus a scenic transfer across the country — say the Dales, the coast, then York to Edinburgh the scenic way.

3

One Yorkshire day tour paired with a scenic transfer — a great way to both see Yorkshire and move on in style.
