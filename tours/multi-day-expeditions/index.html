Multi-Day Private Tours of Yorkshire & Beyond

Pick one of our tours below or have one tailor-made around you. Up to 7 guests travel for one price.

Highlights

Ideal for

Couples, families and small groups who have a few days and want to see more of Britain, without trains, driving or planning.

Expedition Yorkshire's multi-day tours are private, driver-guided trips for up to 7 guests in a Mercedes-Benz V-Class, always with a professional, licensed Expedition Yorkshire guide. Choose the Yorkshire Trilogy (3 days, £2,789), Capital to Capital from London to Edinburgh via York (3 days, £3,750) or the Famous Five London to Edinburgh road trip (5 days, £5,450). Prices are per group and include VAT. Hotels aren't included, but we recommend them, drop you off and collect you each day. Every tour can be tailored, and longer trips across England, Wales and Scotland are planned on request.

Our multi-day tours

Book a tour below as it is, or use it as a starting point. We can swap days, add days or run the route in reverse, and the price always covers your whole group.

3 days · from York

The Yorkshire Trilogy: 3-Day Yorkshire Tour

The most complete Yorkshire experience. Three days, three national parks and a professional driver-guide: the Yorkshire Dales, the North York Moors, the Yorkshire coast and the Peak District with Chatsworth House. You won't drive a mile yourself.

  1. Day 1Yorkshire Dales National Park tour or the All Creatures Great & Small tour: you choose.
  2. Day 2Whitby Moor, Coast & Cliff: moor-top roads, the steam railway at Goathland and Whitby Abbey above the harbour.
  3. Day 3The Peak District National Park, with a visit to Chatsworth House.
£2,789per group, inc VAT · up to 7 travel for the same price

Stay in one York hotel for all three nights, and we'll collect you each morning.

3 days · both directions

Capital to Capital: London to Edinburgh Tour via York

Travel between England's and Scotland's capitals by road instead of rail, with two nights in York on the way. Available London to Edinburgh or Edinburgh to London.

  1. Day 1London to York via Cambridge.
  2. Day 2A two-hour guided walking tour of York, then the rest of the day to explore this beautiful, ancient city at your own pace.
  3. Day 3The classic York to Edinburgh transfer tour.
£3,750per group, inc VAT · up to 7 travel for the same price

We'll drop you at your York hotel on day one. Day two is spent on foot in York, starting with your guided walk, so there's no driving at all. On day three, we collect you from your hotel and drop you at your Edinburgh hotel that evening.

5 days · London to Edinburgh

The Famous Five: 5-Day London to Edinburgh Road Trip

Our most complete trip. London to Edinburgh by road, with York, the Yorkshire Dales and the Whitby coast in between, and four nights in one York hotel so you're not packing every morning.

  1. Day 1London to York via Cambridge.
  2. Day 2A two-hour guided walking tour of York, then a day to explore the city.
  3. Day 3Yorkshire Dales National Park tour or the All Creatures Great & Small tour.
  4. Day 4Whitby Moor, Coast & Cliff.
  5. Day 5The classic York to Edinburgh transfer tour.
£5,450per group, inc VAT · up to 7 travel for the same price

6 days or more · fully tailor-made

If you have more than five days, we'll plan a private tour entirely around you. Guests have gone from the Cotswolds and Wales to the Lake District and the Isle of Skye, with a professional licensed Expedition Yorkshire driver-guide all the way.

Make it yours

Every tour can be tailor-made

The tours above are where most guests start, not where they have to finish. Tell us what you'd love to see, and we'll shape the days around you:

  • Swap a day, for example, Brontë Country instead of the Dales
  • Add days, or leave a free day to rest
  • Reverse the route and finish in London
  • Start from an airport or cruise port
  • Go further: the Lake District, Wales or the Scottish Highlands
  • Bring a bigger group: up to 14 guests across two vehicles, each with its own driver-guide

Tell us your dates, who's coming and what you'd love to see. We'll send one price for your whole group, usually the same day.
