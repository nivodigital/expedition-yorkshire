Ideal for

Fans of the series and the James Herriot books — and anyone who loves classic Dales scenery and villages.

The All Creatures Great & Small tour is a private, full-day driver-guided trip from York for up to 7 guests. You visit the Dales villages, farmsteads and market squares behind the series and James Herriot's stories — Grassington doubles as Darrowby — travelling in a Mercedes-Benz V-Class with hotel pickup. One price covers the whole group, from £985 including VAT.

The day, stop by stop

The places that make the day

Your day could look something like this — the town square, the villages and the farm country in between. It's a guide rather than a fixed timetable: we shape the stops, the order and the pace around your group on the day.

1

Darrowby

Grassington

The cobbled square and shopfronts that become Darrowby on screen — the beating heart of the series.

2

Villages & farms

Dales villages & farmsteads

The lanes, farm gates and stone barns where the vets' rounds are filmed — classic working Dales country.

3

Wensleydale

Wensleydale & the wider Dales

Big-sky drives through the dale, with drystone walls, sheep pasture and the odd tea-room stop along the way.

4

On screen

All Creatures Location

More of the squares and views that appear again and again on screen, at a pace that lets you take your photos.

Big fan of the books? The World of James Herriot in Thirsk can be added on request — just tell your guide.
