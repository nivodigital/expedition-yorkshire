Highlights

Cambridge — colleges, the Backs & world-class museums · a private drive north · door-to-door to your York hotel or station.

Ideal for

Anyone linking London with a Yorkshire stay who'd sooner sightsee than sit on a train.

The London to York transfer tour via Cambridge is a private, door-to-door journey for up to 7 guests in a Mercedes-Benz V-Class. You leave London in the morning, break the drive with lunch and a walking tour of Cambridge, then arrive in York in good time for dinner. One price covers your whole group including VAT, and every mile of the day is built around you.

The journey, stop by stop

A travel day worth having

Your day could look something like this — a private start from London, a proper afternoon in Cambridge, then on into York. It's a guide rather than a fixed timetable: we shape the stops and the pace around your group and your onward plans.

1

Leave the driving to us

A private start from London

We collect you door to door in London and head north in comfort — luggage loaded, the day yours from the very first mile.

2

The highlight stop

Cambridge

Lunch and a walking tour of one of the world's great university cities — the historic colleges, the Backs and optional punting on the Cam. World-class museums like the Fitzwilliam make a perfect wet-weather alternative.

3

Or shape it your way

Stately homes & Duxford

Prefer a stately home or the Imperial War Museum at Duxford to the city? Easily arranged — and we can begin from any London airport (Heathrow, Gatwick, City or Stansted) at no extra charge.

Every route is a guide, not a timetable — tell us your train-free ideal and we'll build the day around it.
